Modules

What your institution contracts, and what comes with it

Four modules are contracted per institution. The member journey is the fifth area: it comes with any contracted module and is not sold separately.

Home Care

Home visits, team rota, supplies at the patient's home, daily route and remote monitoring.

PA Virtual

Risk-classified queue, nurse triage, video visits and follow-up after the encounter.

Artificial Intelligence

It cuts across virtual urgent care, home care and the assistant. Contracted separately, and switched on capability by capability by management.

Analytics

Indicator dashboard for the care operation, so leadership can follow it without requesting a report.

Members

It comes with any contracted module, which is why it is not sold separately: a single registry, passwordless sign-in by WhatsApp code, access released by the team, LGPD consent per purpose, the institution's own scheduling page, conversation history, and notifications by WhatsApp, email and browser.

Turning a module on or off is our operation, never the client's. Contracting or cancelling takes effect immediately, with no release to wait for. What was not contracted does not show in the menu, does not open by direct URL and does not respond: in doubt, the system closes instead of allowing.

Three controls stack on the same request, and none replaces another: the person's role, the contracted module, and the data separation per institution.

Doctor-to-doctor second opinion is not a separate module. It comes with virtual urgent care and switches on together with it.

Entry channel

Elderly patients will not install an app, but they do use WhatsApp

Identification, consent, pre-triage and the queue ticket all happen inside the conversation. The only screen that opens in a browser is the video room. That removes the app, the password and the portal address from the path, which is exactly where elderly members give up.

The channel runs on the official WhatsApp API, contracted by the institution itself directly with Meta. Each institution serves patients from its own number, and that number is not shared with any other.

From the message to the video room

  • Whoever is writing is checked against the institution's registry
  • LGPD consent accepted in the conversation, with the exact text on record
  • Pre-triage inside the same dialogue
  • A red-flag symptom ends the questions, prioritises the case and calls nursing right away
  • Queue ticket and video link delivered at the end of the conversation
  • Passwordless sign-in, with the code sent in the conversation itself

Scheduling and history in the same channel

  • Book, reschedule and cancel inside the conversation
  • Only the person identified in the conversation can touch the schedule
  • Explicit confirmation before cancelling
  • History per member: what was sent, what was answered, and whether it was delivered
  • Search by date range and by keyword, with export
  • Visible on both the home care and the patient record

About switching the channel on. Sending and receiving messages are two different settings. With the first one, the system already sends access codes and notifications. The care conversation depends on the second, and the integrations screen reports both states separately, so nobody assumes the whole channel is live.

Remote monitoring

Your team is the one who sets the clinical threshold

Vital signs and validated scales feed alert rules that management configures on screen. The team is warned before the patient deteriorates, without waiting for the next visit to notice.

Alert rules defined by management

Risk type, monitored parameter, cut-off range, severity and message are all defined on screen. A published rule does not change afterwards, every alert stores the version that produced it, and a dashboard shows how often each rule fires. Changing a clinical threshold does not depend on calling the vendor.

Artificial Intelligence

The AI suggests, the clinician decides, and the data does not travel

Seven capabilities, switched on one by one by management. None of them writes into the record on its own: the output comes back to the screen marked as a suggestion and only becomes a clinical entry when the clinician saves it.

Seven capabilities, switched on one by one

  • Transcription of the recorded visit
  • SOAP notes from the transcription
  • Clinical summary of the history
  • Suggested diagnosis codes and tests
  • Adaptive pre-triage
  • Admission risk prediction
  • Fraud detection
  • Admission risk prediction and fraud detection list their reasons, one by one, over data the institution already holds. It is not a trained-model black box

Clinical data does not leave the network

  • The model runs on the institution's own infrastructure, with Ollama, and not on a third-party service
  • The system refuses to start if the model address points outside the local network
  • A specific AI consent, refusable without blocking care
  • It never diagnoses or prescribes on its own, by product design

AI cuts across the modules and is sold separately. Without the module contracted, AI controls disappear from the screen instead of sitting greyed out, the assistant stops conversing, its panel becomes a support channel, and pre-triage goes back to nursing. Even with the internal switches on: between switch and contract, the contract wins.

Integrations

What already runs, and what still depends on a contract

The two groups are kept apart on purpose. Selling a finished interface as a connected vendor is the kind of thing that surfaces in the first meeting with your IT team.

Already working, they only need the environment configured

  • Official WhatsApp API, with sending and receiving configured separately
  • Email and browser notifications
  • Ollama, serving the assistant and the clinical AI inside the network
  • Speech transcription on your own infrastructure
  • A TURN server, so video crosses a restricted corporate network
  • Route planning with visit order and street distance
  • Corporate SSO over OpenID Connect per institution: Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta and Keycloak
  • Zammad para chamados de suporte
  • Monitoring and logs: Sentry, Grafana, Loki and Promtail
  • Brazil's national health data network (RNDS) with clinical encounter records in FHIR R4

Interface ready, connection depending on contract and certification

  • Tasy
  • TISS
  • PACS, LIS and RIS
  • SMS
  • ICP-Brasil digital signature
  • A ready interface does not mean a connected vendor. Each one needs its own address, credentials, contract and certification

About corporate sign-in. The provider is configured per institution, never one for all of them. A single provider would let any directory account into any client, because the sign-in does not say which institution the person works for. With no recognised group in the directory map, access is refused instead of falling back to a default profile.

About the national health data network. Accreditation belongs to the healthcare facility, not to the platform: it requires an active CNES registration and an ICP-Brasil e-CNPJ certificate, filed on the DATASUS service portal, with the platform declared as the system in the request.

Architecture

For the IT team that will audit the product

Each area holds its own business rules and talks to the others only through a defined interface. A change in one module is less likely to break another, and a technical audit finds clear boundaries instead of tangled code.

44 isolated backend domains

Communication only through defined interfaces, across 268 API endpoints. New work extends the matching domain, without rewriting the platform.

Node.js and Express on PostgreSQL 16

Frontend in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no framework. A light page is a field requirement, not an optional optimisation: home care works on phones and unstable networks.

Docker Compose and observability

It comes up without a complex orchestrator, with a Grafana, Loki and Promtail overlay, Sentry, and runbooks for backup and continuity.

Automated test suite

Unit, API and end-to-end tests with Playwright, covering business rules, encryption and isolation between institutions.

Security and data protection

Compliance starting from the data design

Separation guaranteed by the database, an audit trail that exposes a record altered after the fact, and consent that stores the exact text the person read. These are the questions compliance and the data protection authority ask, and the ones that cost dearly without an answer.

Each institution's data separated by the database

Row level security across 60 PostgreSQL tables. A query without the filter sees no row from another institution, instead of leaking the data. The application check stays active on top of it, as a second barrier.

Audit trail with a hash chain

Every entry chains to the previous one, so touching the past becomes detectable. It covers sign-in, records, prescriptions, attachment downloads and recordings. National IDs and clinical text never enter the log detail.

Sensitive fields encrypted

AES-256-GCM, with an HMAC search index so the screen stays searchable. Losing the key makes the data unrecoverable, so it lives in a vault, with its own backup, outside the repository.

Session, role and consent

Session, role, job title and second factor on internal routes. Consent per purpose that archives the exact text accepted, and record retention under Brazilian medical council resolution CFM 1.821/2007.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA is the internal target the product works towards, not a certification already obtained. On the member's screen it becomes big buttons, big text and plain language.

Frequently asked questions

What medical leadership, IT and compliance ask before signing

Does member clinical data leave the institution's network?

No. The AI model runs on the institution's own infrastructure, and the system refuses to start if the model address points outside the local network.

Does the AI decide a diagnosis or a prescription?

No. The AI suggests and the clinician decides. Every output comes back marked as a suggestion and only becomes a clinical entry when the clinician approves it.

How does isolation between institutions work?

Each institution sees only its own data, and the database is what guarantees it, through PostgreSQL row level security across 60 tables. A query without the filter sees no row from another institution, instead of leaking the data.

Does care over WhatsApp require an app?

No. Identification, consent, pre-triage and the queue ticket all happen inside the conversation itself. The only browser screen is the video room.

Can anyone enter virtual urgent care?

No. Only members already registered in home care get in, with access released by the team. That is a product decision, not a technical limitation.

Does the team sign in with the institution's corporate account?

Yes, against the institution's own directory, over OpenID Connect: Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta and Keycloak. The provider is configured per institution, and with no recognised group in the directory map access is refused.

Does LarClínica integrate with existing hospital systems?

There are ready interfaces for Tasy, TISS, PACS, LIS and RIS, and the national health data network already operates with clinical encounter records in FHIR R4. Actually connecting each vendor depends on their address, credentials and certification.
