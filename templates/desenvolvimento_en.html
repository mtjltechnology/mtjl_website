How we work

Testing is not the last phase of the project

MTJL started out as a software quality consultancy. That changes how we build: tests come in together with the code, not after the delivery is already standing and nobody wants to touch it anymore. And because development, quality and AI sit with the same supplier, responsibility is not fragmented when something fails or needs to evolve.

Domain before screens

First understand the business rules and how they break down. Each area becomes a module with a clear boundary, so changing one thing does not force a rewrite of the platform. That lowers the risk of integrating with a legacy system, because the connection stays contained in one module, and it makes technical review by the customer's IT team easier.

Automated testing from the start

Unit, integration and full journey tests running in the pipeline. You do not need to hire the quality part separately, it already comes with it.

Fail loudly, not silently

Incomplete or insecure configuration brings the application down at startup instead of leaving it running degraded. A visible error costs far less than a hidden one.

Delivered with a manual

Operations documentation, backup routine and a tested restore. You should be able to run the system without depending on whoever wrote it.

Product owner experience

We are not only a project supplier. We keep our own products running, so we know what a bad architecture decision costs in the second year, not on delivery day.

Technical transparency as a principle

We state clearly what is already done and what depends on a third-party contract or credential. You do not discover a blocking dependency the day before going live.
