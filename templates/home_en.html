About MTJL Technology

A strategic partner in technology and AI

We are a technology and AI company based in Curitiba, Brazil, specializing in custom software development, quality automation, software testing, and products powered by Generative Artificial Intelligence. We serve clients in Brazil and the United States, across Technology, Healthcare, Financial services and Industry.

In practice, that happens across three complementary fronts. We build custom web applications and APIs, with architecture designed to scale alongside the client's business. In parallel, we act as a software quality consultancy, from diagnosing a team's current testing process to implementing automation suites integrated into the deployment pipeline, covering manual, regression, performance, and security testing along the way. And we apply AI on two fronts. We build agents that serve customers and run internal processes. Already in salons, healthcare, scheduling, supermarkets, and orders. And we train custom models, from scratch or by fine-tuning an existing one, for object detection, image classification, and test automation. RelatifyAI Beauty is one of those agents: it serves salons and barbershops on WhatsApp 24 hours a day.
